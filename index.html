South Korea

My first international trip, in Spring 2024, reshaped how I see technology, discipline, and culture. Our guide arrived five minutes early every single day, and everywhere we went, working people and schoolchildren chose to walk or take the subway over driving—a reminder that health and fitness are valued as part of everyday life.

Visiting the temples was mesmerising, each one carrying its own story and significance, and it was the warmth of the people that made the country feel truly homely and safe, leaving me with memories to cherish.
